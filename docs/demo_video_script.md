# JARVIS Beta Launch Demo Video

**Target Length:** 90 - 120 seconds  
**Objective:** Demonstrate the core promise: JARVIS makes glanceable information, quick actions, and spatial organization feel faster and more enjoyable. Prove it works seamlessly with mouse and keyboard as fallbacks, and emphasize privacy.

---

### Sequence 1: The Setup (0:00 - 0:15)
- **Visuals:** Open on a clean, empty browser window. The user goes to `jarvis.localhost`. The sleek, dark onboarding flow appears. 
- **Action:** User quickly clicks through the onboarding flow. 
- **Voiceover/Text:** "JARVIS is a spatial command center that lives entirely in your browser."
- **Visuals:** The void materializes with the Obsidian core and the glowing HoloDecor grid. The boot scene windows float smoothly into view (News, Weather).
- **Voiceover/Text:** "It's built for speed, organization, and deeply enjoyable interactions."

### Sequence 2: Spatial Control (0:15 - 0:45)
- **Visuals:** The user raises their hand. The blue glowing cursor appears instantly. They hover over the News window and pinch to grab it, tossing it to the right with fluid momentum.
- **Voiceover/Text:** "Use your hands to arrange your workspace effortlessly. Our custom physics engine makes it feel physical and responsive."
- **Visuals:** User brings up the LLM Chat window and the Settings window using the gesture launcher. They do a two-finger pinch-to-zoom to resize the Chat window to be much larger.
- **Voiceover/Text:** "Pinch to zoom, swipe to scroll. It's the tactile control of a touchscreen, built for mid-air."

### Sequence 3: Privacy & Fallbacks (0:45 - 1:00)
- **Visuals:** The user drops their hands. They grab the mouse and seamlessly drag a window using the mouse cursor. They use keyboard shortcuts (Shift+F) to enter Focus Mode.
- **Voiceover/Text:** "But gestures never trap you. Mouse and keyboard are always complete fallbacks."
- **Visuals:** Camera pans slightly while the user looks at the HUD. The HUD shows "Hands: 0", but the latency and FPS are steady.
- **Voiceover/Text:** "And privacy is paramount. Hand tracking runs 100% locally on your device. We never see your camera feed."

### Sequence 4: Collaborative Presence (1:00 - 1:20)
- **Visuals:** A second colored cursor (e.g., magenta) appears on screen. The other user grabs a window and moves it. The HUD shows `Sync Users: 2`.
- **Voiceover/Text:** "When you're ready, share your void. Multi-user sync lets you collaborate spatially in real-time."
- **Visuals:** The first user points at a specific card to highlight it.
- **Voiceover/Text:** "It's not about replacing your screen. It's about giving your information space to breathe."

### Sequence 5: Outro (1:20 - 1:30)
- **Visuals:** The user presses Shift+R to reset the view. The scene returns to the pristine boot layout. Fade to black with the JARVIS logo.
- **Voiceover/Text:** "JARVIS Closed Beta. Enter the void."
